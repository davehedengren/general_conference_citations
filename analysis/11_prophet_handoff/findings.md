# 11 — Prophet Handoff Transition

**Question.** In the 8-conference windows around each presidency transition, do citation patterns shift immediately, or is there a lag/eulogy effect?

## Results (BoM share, 8-conference means pre vs post transition)

| transition | outgoing → incoming | pre BoM | post BoM | Δ (pp) | NT Δ |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1972-10 | Smith → Lee | 12.9 % | 14.7 % | +1.8 | −3.1 |
| 1974-04 | Lee → Kimball | 13.3 % | 15.6 % | +2.3 | +0.4 |
| **1986-04** | **Kimball → Benson** | **22.7 %** | **34.8 %** | **+12.0** | **−9.3** |
| 1994-10 | Benson → Hunter | 32.3 % | 25.7 % | **−6.5** | +3.5 |
| 1995-04 | Hunter → Hinckley | 31.3 % | 26.2 % | **−5.1** | +3.1 |
| 2008-04 | Hinckley → Monson | 34.4 % | 32.7 % | −1.8 | +1.0 |
| **2018-04** | **Monson → Nelson** | **34.4 %** | **37.9 %** | **+3.5** | −4.0 |

## Takeaways
- **Benson transition (1986)**: pulpit swung hard toward BoM (+12 pp, largest in the dataset) and away from NT (−9 pp) — almost immediately. No lag.
- **Post-Benson contraction** (1994 and 1995 transitions): BoM share dropped ~6 pp across both brief Hunter and early Hinckley windows. Conference reverted toward the NT center of gravity before Hinckley's later BoM re-emphasis.
- **Nelson transition (2018)**: +3.5 pp BoM jump, mirrors his personal fingerprint; no eulogy-style lag for Monson.
- The two earliest transitions (Smith/Lee/Kimball) show only modest moves, but come from a low BoM baseline.

**No clear "eulogy effect"** — shifts happen quickly, not gradually. Transitions to prophets with distinct personal fingerprints (Benson, Nelson) produce abrupt conference-wide swings.

# 01 — Curriculum Effect on Citations

**Question.** Does the year's book-of-study (Sunday School / Come Follow Me) lift citations to that book in conference during that year?

**Method.** A 4-year rotation (OT → NT → BoM → D&C) is assumed, anchored at 2024 = BoM. For each conference we compute the share of citations going to each book and compare years when a book is "in the curriculum" vs. all other years.

## Key results

### Full range 1971–2025
Lift is essentially zero across all books (≤ ±0.7 pp).

| book | in-curriculum share | out-of-curriculum share | lift (pp) |
|------|---------------------|-------------------------|-----------|
| BoM  | 28.8 %              | 28.8 %                  | −0.01     |
| D&C  | 24.9 %              | 24.6 %                  | +0.31     |
| NT   | 29.7 %              | 30.4 %                  | −0.73     |
| OT   | 11.0 %              | 10.7 %                  | +0.29     |

### Come-Follow-Me era (2019–2025)
A visible BoM bump emerges once CFM standardizes a home-centered cycle:

| book | in | out | lift (pp) |
|------|----|-----|-----------|
| **BoM** | **39.9 %** | **36.4 %** | **+3.5** |
| D&C  | 20.6 %      | 20.8 %      | −0.1      |
| NT   | 28.2 %      | 27.8 %      | +0.4      |
| OT   | 7.2 %       | 9.1 %       | −1.9      |

### Takeaway
- Before 2019, the pulpit does **not** track the Sunday-School book of study.
- Post-CFM there is a **~3.5 pp BoM lift** in BoM-years (2020, 2024). OT actually *drops* in its own year — OT years in 2022 show strong NT citation growth, which may reflect unrelated Christ-centering trend.
- Sample is thin (14 conferences), and 2020 BoM year coincides with COVID pivot. Treat as suggestive.

### Caveats
- Curriculum rotation pre-2019 is approximate (Gospel Doctrine / home study).
- Confound: prophetic emphasis on BoM (Nelson-era) overlaps with CFM era.

# 08 — Christ-Centering Index (NT + BoM share)

**Question.** Has General Conference become more Christ-centered (measured crudely by the combined share of citations from the two volumes most focused on Christ: NT + BoM)?

## Time series highlights

| year | NT share | BoM share | **NT+BoM** |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1971 | 35.3 % | 12.9 % | **48.1 %** |
| 1980 | 30.4 % | 13.4 % | **43.8 %** (low) |
| 1990 | 26.5 % | 29.2 % | 55.7 % |
| 2000 | 29.1 % | 33.5 % | 62.6 % |
| 2010 | 28.0 % | 31.5 % | 59.6 % |
| 2018 | 28.8 % | 36.5 % | 65.3 % |
| 2020 | 21.3 % | 41.2 % | 62.6 % |
| 2023 | 33.1 % | 34.8 % | **68.7 % (peak)** |
| 2025 | 31.5 % | 35.9 % | 67.3 % |

## Takeaways
- **Secular rise**: the index climbed from ~44 % in 1980 to **~68 %** in the mid-2020s — roughly a **24-point** expansion.
- The rise is **entirely BoM-driven** until ~2020; NT share has been flat-to-declining over the full range.
- 2023 is the **peak** (68.7 %), with NT re-emerging — consistent with the 2023 NT curriculum year.
- The low (1980, 43.8 %) falls in the mid-Kimball era before Benson's BoM emphasis.

## Caveats
- "Christ-centering" is a crude label — D&C contains extensive Christ-teachings too. An alternative index combining NT+BoM+portions of D&C could be constructed but requires verse-level data.
